About Kamwokya Weyonje Hub

Grassroots sanitation awareness rooted in community pride, health protection, and affordable disposal.

Why "Weyonje"?

"Weyonje" is a Luganda word meaning "clean up yourself" or "keep your surroundings clean." We chose this name to promote local ownership across Kamwokya II Parish—reminding every household, shopkeeper, and market vendor that a clean environment starts with personal responsibility.

Why Illegal Dumping is Harmful to Kamwokya

In Kampala, only about 60% of the 1,500 tons of solid waste generated daily is collected by municipal authorities (Daily Monitor, March 14, 2024). Because private garbage collectors charge 25,000 to 30,000 UGX per month, many low-income residents dump waste into drainage channels and vacant plots at night. This creates severe community hazards:

  • Flash Flooding: Plastic bottles and polythene bags clog drainage trenches, forcing contaminated rainwater into homes and market stalls during heavy rains.
  • Waterborne & Vector Diseases: Stagnant water trapped by garbage breeds mosquitoes that spread malaria, while rotting waste attracts flies and rats linked to cholera and typhoid.
  • Market & Livelihood Losses: Foul odors and overflowing trash behind Kamwokya Market drive away customers and threaten food hygiene.

Affordable Legal Alternatives

Residents do not need to dump waste in trenches. Through our 1,000 UGX Skip Directory, families can dispose of household waste legally at KCCA-subsidized community skips for just 1,000 UGX per sack.
